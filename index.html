03 / Journey

Learning
by going deeper.

There is no straight line from curiosity to understanding. The path usually looks more like a collection of problems, experiments and unexpected connections.

PHASE 01 — THE QUESTION

Start with “Why?”

A simple question is often more valuable than a quick answer. The goal is not merely to solve a problem, but to understand why the solution works.

PHASE 02 — THE PROBLEM

Go deeper.

Olympiad mathematics provides the perfect laboratory: problems that resist obvious approaches and reward structure, creativity and persistence.

PHASE 03 — THE CONNECTION

Find the hidden bridge.

Geometry connects to algebra. Algorithms connect to mathematics. A visual pattern becomes a theorem. The most interesting discoveries often happen between subjects.

PHASE 04 — THE NEXT PROBLEM

Never really finished.

Solving one problem rarely ends the story. It usually creates three better questions. That is where the next idea begins.

“The goal is not to know everything. It is to keep finding better questions.”
— Personal principle
